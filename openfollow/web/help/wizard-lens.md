# Lens

A wide lens bows straight lines. The Lens step measures how much, so the Operator Screen's overlay bows the same way.

## Lines

Each line you trace must be straight in reality. The dashed suggestions are edges the station found in the snapshot; whether one is straight in reality is yours to judge. The fit makes all of the lines straight at once. Lines near the edges and corners of the picture tell the most.

A clicked or dragged point stays selected, with the loupe on it, and the arrow keys nudge it a pixel at a time (**Shift** for ten); **Tab** selects one too. **Enter**, **Esc** or a click anywhere else ends the selection. **Point off** or **Space** switches a selected middle point off or on; a middle point that found no edge starts switched off. A click on a line selects the whole line; **Delete** or **Backspace** removes the selected line, and a line just added is selected, so the key takes it back.

## Coverage

The five-step rating says how well the lines pin the lens down. A **Low** result is shown but not applied. **Edge fit** is only measured once a line reaches a corner of the picture.

## Load and save

**Load lens calibration** sets the lens from a calibration saved on this station, or from the lens values of an exported `.ofsettings` file; nothing else in the file is applied. Use it when the picture has no straight edge to trace, with the same camera and lens at the same zoom. Loading removes the traced lines; the Fine-tune values can still be changed afterwards.

**Save lens calibration** stores the current values on this station under a name, for this camera and lens later. A name that is already saved is replaced.

## Full screen

The button in the picture's top right corner shows it on the whole screen, with one line above it: the next thing to do, a line that does not fit, **Point off**, **Delete line**, **?** for how to use it and its keyboard shortcuts, **Space** among them, and **Exit full screen**. Everything works there as on the page. **Esc** ends full screen and cancels an active selection, an open menu or a started line.
