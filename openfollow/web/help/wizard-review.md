# Review

**Save and finish** saves the camera, the lens and the points. In Simple mode it also saves the rectangle as the grid. The green grid is the one the Operator Screen will draw.

**Leave without saving** keeps the station's camera, lens and grid as they were.

**Save and finish**, **Leave without saving** and **← Back to Settings** all return to the General tab.

Every point is saved, so **Save and finish** stays off while a point is not complete, such as a known point without a value or a triangulated point without a pin. Review names each of these points and what it needs. **Points** and **Pinned** count what **Save and finish** saves.

**Camera height** is listed when the camera looks almost straight down or a height was entered: the height the calculation kept, or **Not entered**.

## Floor height

Advanced only. The grid's Z Offset: the floor that a marker's Z line, ground circle and cone are drawn down to, and that a mouse drag moves a marker on. Width, depth and offsets stay as set on the Grid tab.

It starts at the height that the most points share, within 5 cm, and the note names those points. When no two points share a height, or two separate groups are the same size, it starts at 0. When the grid is at another height now, the note says so. The green grid follows the value as you type.
